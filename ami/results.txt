# AI in Global Politics

**One-sentence summary:**
AI is reshaping geopolitics through autonomous weapons development, election interference capabilities, and great power competition between the US, China, and EU over standard-setting and dominance.

**Key developments:**

1. **Great power AI race** – US-China competition for AI supremacy mirrors Cold War dynamics; China leads in some applications (facial recognition, surveillance), US in others (large language models, semiconductors)

2. **Governance vacuum** – No binding international AI regulations exist; UN, G7, and bilateral talks are underway but enforcement remains unclear

3. **Election & information warfare** – Deepfakes, chatbots, and algorithmic disinformation are now standard tools; 2024 elections saw significant AI-generated content manipulation

4. **Autonomous weapons concerns** – Military AI deployment (drones, targeting systems) is accelerating without clear rules; killer robot treaties remain unsigned

**Specific searches:**
- "AI arms control negotiations" – treaties and proposals in motion
- "China vs US AI dominance" – competitive landscape and implications
- "Algorithmic disinformation 2024 elections" – concrete recent examples

# AI in Global Politics

**Current State:** AI is reshaping political power dynamics, election integrity, and geopolitical competition, with no global governance framework yet in place.

## Key Developments

1. **Great Power Competition** – US, China, and EU are in an AI race with geopolitical implications; China leads in surveillance AI, US in military applications, EU in regulation-first approach

2. **Election Interference** – Deepfakes, disinformation bots, and AI-generated content are becoming scalable tools for manipulating elections and public opinion across democracies

3. **Autonomous Weapons & Military AI** – Nations are developing lethal autonomous weapons with minimal human oversight, raising both strategic instability and humanitarian concerns

4. **Regulatory Fragmentation** – EU's AI Act, US sector-specific rules, and China's content controls create a splintered global landscape affecting tech governance
